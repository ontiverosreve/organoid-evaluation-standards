Two supplementary derived files are stored base64-encoded because the push
tool cannot carry large/binary arguments:

- fs369_stim_cohort.csv.gz.b64  ->  base64 -d ... | gunzip  ->  fs369_stim_cohort.csv
  (464 KB derived stim-cohort table; gzip'd to ~13 KB)
- fs369_trial_features.npz.b64  ->  base64 -d ... > fs369_trial_features.npz
  (120-trial feature arrays: X_full, X_base, y, day, amp)

Both decode losslessly; checksums verified at push time.
